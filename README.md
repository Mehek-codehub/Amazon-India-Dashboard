# Amazon India Sales Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Amazon India Sales Dashboard** is an interactive Excel-based business dashboard designed to provide a clear and easy-to-understand overview of business performance.

The dashboard brings important business information together in one place, helping users quickly understand **Sales, Profit, Products, Orders, Customer Order Status, Payment, Fulfillment, and Geographic Performance**.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objective of this project is to create a simple management dashboard that allows non-technical users to understand business performance through **KPIs, charts, graphs, and trends**.

The dashboard helps monitor:

- Sales performance
- Profitability
- Product and category performance
- Order status
- Returns and cancellations
- Payment methods
- Fulfillment methods
- Geographic performance

## 📌 Key KPIs

The dashboard includes the following key metrics:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Units Sold
- Average Order Value
- Profit Margin %
- Return Rate %
- Cancellation Rate %

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Overall Sales & Profit Performance

This section focuses on overall business performance.

**Metrics and analysis:**
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Units Sold
- Average Order Value
- Profit Margin %
- Monthly Sales & Profit Trend

This helps identify whether sales and profitability are improving or declining over time.

### 2. Category & Product Performance

This section analyzes which products and categories are contributing to business performance.

**Analysis includes:**
- Category-wise Sales
- Category-wise Profit
- Category-wise Units Sold
- Top 10 Products by Sales

This helps identify high-performing products and categories that may require attention.

### 3. Order Status & Revenue Loss

This section analyzes completed and potentially lost orders.

**Analysis includes:**
- Delivered Orders
- Shipped Orders
- Returned Orders
- Cancelled Orders
- Return Rate %
- Cancellation Rate %
- Category-wise Returns
- Category-wise Cancellations

This provides visibility into the impact of returns and cancellations on business performance.

### 4. Payment, Fulfillment & Geographic Performance

This section evaluates payment methods, fulfillment methods, and geographic performance.

**Payment Analysis:**
- Sales by Payment Method
- Profit by Payment Method

**Fulfillment Analysis:**
- Sales by Fulfillment Method
- Profit by Fulfillment Method

**Geographic Analysis:**
- State-wise Sales
- State-wise Profit
- State-wise Orders

This helps understand important markets and how different payment and fulfillment channels contribute to business performance.

## 💡 Key Business Questions

The dashboard answers questions such as:

1. How is the business performing overall?
2. Which products and categories are driving sales?
3. How much profit is being generated?
4. How many orders are delivered, shipped, returned, or cancelled?
5. What is the return and cancellation rate?
6. Which payment methods generate sales and profit?
7. Which fulfillment methods contribute to business performance?
8. Which states generate the highest sales, profit, and orders?
